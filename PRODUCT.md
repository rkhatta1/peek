# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Peek is primarily for a consultancy operator supervising client infrastructure without access to the client machine. The user is usually checking several environments quickly, diagnosing a warning, or verifying that a client system remains comfortably within its operating limits.

## Product Purpose

Peek collects and normalizes Neon Postgres and Upstash Redis health, usage, and performance signals into one external control surface. Success means the operator can spot meaningful changes early, understand which provider or environment is responsible, and move from alert to evidence without exposing client credentials or sensitive query data.

## Positioning

Operational evidence for client data infrastructure, without living inside every client stack.

## Brand Personality

Clean, optimized, and well-engineered. Peek should feel calm under pressure: concise enough for routine scanning, precise enough for incident work, and visibly built by people who care about systems quality.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative SaaS dashboards, oversized vanity metrics, glass-heavy surfaces, raw terminal cosplay, and dense enterprise control panels that make every signal look equally important. Avoid UI that implies precision when a provider has returned stale, unavailable, or incomplete telemetry.

## Design Principles

- Lead with operational state, then expose the evidence behind it.
- Keep routine scanning quiet; reserve visual intensity for conditions that require action.
- Make freshness, source, environment, and uncertainty explicit.
- Prefer fast, familiar controls over novel dashboard interactions.
- Protect client data by design: aggregate, redact, and keep credentials server-side.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Status must never depend on color alone, interactive targets must remain usable by keyboard and touch, data displays need textual equivalents, and all non-essential motion must respect reduced-motion preferences.
